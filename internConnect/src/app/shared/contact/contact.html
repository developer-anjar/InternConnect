Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions asked by students and companies

What is InternConnect?

InternConnect is a platform that helps students find verified internships and lets companies hire talented interns easily.

How do students apply for internships?

Students can explore opportunities, click “Apply Now,” and submit details directly. Companies review applications instantly.

Can companies post internships?

Yes. Registered companies get a dashboard to add, manage, and review internship applications.

Is InternConnect free for students?

Absolutely! Students can explore and apply for internships completely free of cost.

How can I contact support?

Visit our Contact Page to reach us by email or phone.
